Tokenfront account deletion request

Upcoming billing feature — draft. The billing backend is not yet deployed and purchases are disabled. This page is not a live authenticated deletion service. No public deployment URL is claimed here.

Request help without the app

Email korea@toris.kr with the subject “Tokenfront account deletion request”. Explain that you want your Tokenfront billing account deleted and whether you can access the app. We must verify ownership before deleting anything; sending an email alone does not authenticate you or complete deletion. Never email passwords, sign-in tokens, verification codes or full purchase tokens. There is no Google sign-in on this page.

In-app route when billing is enabled

  1. Open Tokenfront Settings and sign in with the Google account used for billing.
  2. Choose Delete billing account.
  3. Read the warning, explicitly confirm, and complete fresh Google verification using the same account.
  4. Wait for confirmation. If the result is unknown, retry promptly without switching accounts; if retry is unavailable or the app was closed, contact the address above.

What deletion removes

The active billing database removes the Tokenfront account binding and associated purchase verification records, encrypted purchase tokens, token hashes, order IDs and account-linked verification locks and rate counters. Account-linked login nonce metadata is detached; random replay-prevention digests remain temporarily until expiry cleanup. API sessions for the deleted binding no longer authenticate. No personal account tombstone is kept in the active database.

After server acknowledgement, the app ends ad removal and clears its local signed entitlement. The purchase restoration link ends: restoration is not guaranteed after deletion, including after signing in again. This is not a Google Play refund and does not delete your Google account or Google / Google Play transaction records. Contact Google Play separately about refunds. Local game progress and settings are separate from the billing account and are not erased by this action.

Limits and retention

If local storage cleanup fails, the app reports that the server account was deleted but local cleanup failed, stops granting the benefit in the current process and retries. Keep it open and retry. Data may remain on disk if writes fail. Signed offline grants already stored on another device, or recovered after failed disk cleanup and restart, can remain valid until their original expiry (up to 30 days); the server cannot remotely erase an offline device.

Deletion from the active database does not instantly erase provider backups, disaster-recovery snapshots or infrastructure logs. Backup retention, deletion-request email retention and a safe restore procedure must be finalized before billing launch; no verified retention deadline is available yet. Support requests are handled separately from the billing database. Do not interpret an in-app confirmation as proof that Google or every backup has been erased.

계정 삭제 요청

결제 기능은 아직 출시되지 않았습니다. 앱을 이용할 수 없으면 korea@toris.kr로 Tokenfront 계정 삭제를 요청하세요. 소유권 확인이 필요하며 이메일 전송만으로 삭제되지 않습니다. 출시 후 앱 설정 → 결제 계정 삭제에서 경고를 확인하고 동일한 Google 계정을 다시 인증하세요. 광고 제거 혜택과 복원 연결이 종료되며 복원은 보장되지 않습니다. Google Play 환불이나 Google 기록 삭제가 아닙니다. 백업 및 오프라인 기기 데이터는 즉시 삭제되지 않을 수 있습니다.

アカウント削除のリクエスト

課金機能は未公開です。アプリを利用できない場合は korea@toris.kr にお問い合わせください。所有権の確認が必要で、メール送信だけでは削除されません。公開後は設定 → 課金アカウントを削除から警告を確認し、同じGoogleアカウントで再認証してください。広告削除特典と復元の紐付けが終了し、復元は保証されません。Google Playの払い戻しやGoogleの記録削除ではありません。バックアップやオフライン端末のデータは直ちに消去されない場合があります。

申请删除账户

付费功能尚未上线。无法使用应用时，请联系 korea@toris.kr。需要验证账户所有权，仅发送邮件不会完成删除。上线后可在设置 → 删除付费账户中确认警告，并重新验证同一个Google账户。去广告权益和恢复关联将终止，不保证能够恢复。这不是Google Play退款，也不会删除Google记录。备份和离线设备数据可能不会立即清除。
